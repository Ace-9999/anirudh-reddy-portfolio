CTF infrastructure

CyberArena

Built a practice and competition platform for junior students and deployed it on AWS EC2. Students sign in with their VIT Google account and launch personal Docker instances. Each participant gets a unique flag, and solves update the leaderboard through Socket.IO. Organizers use a Flask console to schedule events, manage access, and freeze standings.

React · Flask · Docker · MariaDB · Redis · Socket.IO

Research & machine learning

Ongoing ICU time-series research

AI disease timeline prediction

Predicting how disease unfolds in intensive care: investigating how the order of sepsis and kidney dysfunction relates to survival in a cohort of 365 ICU stays. The pipeline holds out entire stays and excludes future events from onset features. Five-fold baselines are complete. Deep-model comparisons and evaluation on an external cohort remain in progress.

Time series · Survival analysis · Validation
